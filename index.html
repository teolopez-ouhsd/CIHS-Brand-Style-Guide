02 Clear Space & Sizex = height of the CHANNEL ISLANDS line

Clear Space

Keep the area around the logo free of text, photos, other logos, and the edge of the page so it can breathe and stay legible.

xxxx

Minimum clear space on every side equals x, the height of the letters in CHANNEL ISLANDS (the small top line of the stacked word mark). For marks without lettering, such as the Raider Head, Raider Body, ship, or monogram, use one quarter of the mark's width. When the logo is resized, the clear space scales with it.

03 Usage Rules4 do8 don't

Protect the Marks

Proportions, colors, and construction are fixed. If you find yourself dragging a corner handle, changing a fill color, or adding an effect, stop and grab a different file from the library instead.

✓ Do

Use the full-color Raider Head on white or light backgrounds whenever color printing is available.

✓ Do

On Royal Blue, switch to a one-color gold mark. Gold on Royal Blue passes contrast; the full-color blue hat would disappear.

✓ Do

Use the Raider Body for tall spaces: banners, hallway graphics, and spirit art.

✓ Do

Use the CI Monogram for square spaces: social avatars, stickers, patches, and app icons.

✕ Don't

Stretch, squash, or distort. Hold Shift when resizing.

✕ Don't

Recolor. Blue, gold, mocha, gray, and black only, as supplied.

✕ Don't

Add shadows, glows, outlines, bevels, or other effects.

✕ Don't

Rotate or tilt the logo.

✕ Don't

Put word marks on Royal Blue or dark backgrounds. The blue letters have no outline and disappear.

Base vs. tints

Base colors are for primary brand moments: logos, headers, uniforms, signage. Shades and tints are for supporting work such as web UI, charts, table fills, and backgrounds. For print, match the official Pantone or CMYK values.

Pantone

Pantone colors are official. Give print vendors the PMS number for spot-color jobs and the CMYK values for 4-color printing. On-screen colors may not match Pantone standards exactly; consult a current Pantone guide for color-critical work.

Black & gray

The black, grayscale, and black-and-white logo versions use the black and gray values built into those files. They are for one-ink and photocopy jobs, so they aren't part of the brand palette.

06 AccessibilityWCAG 2.24.5:1 body · 3:1 large text & UI

Contrast

WCAG requires at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal body text, and at least 3:1 for large text (18px and up, or 14px bold) and UI elements like icons, buttons, and input borders. Ratios below are calculated from the official HEX values.

ColorAs text on whiteAs text on blackWhite text on itBlack text on it
Never

Light Blue text on Royal Blue, or Gold on Light Blue. They measure about 1.9:1 and 2.4:1, well under the 3:1 minimum. Separate them with white, black, or plenty of space.

Avoid

Gold and Mocha as text on white (2.0:1 and 1.9:1). Use them for accents, rules, and icons beside text, never for the words themselves. Both read well on black, and both pass AA on Royal Blue (4.7:1 and 4.9:1).

  • Royal Blue on white passes AAA (9.5:1). Use it for headings, body links, and buttons with white text.
  • Light Blue on white passes AA (4.9:1), so it works for links, and for buttons with white text.
  • Gold text on Royal Blue passes AA (4.7:1). It is the strongest brand pairing for headlines on dark blue.
  • Avoid light tints behind white text; use them as subtle fills behind dark text.
  • Underline links regardless of color, so color isn't the only signal.

Open Sans

Paragraphs, captions, letters, forms, tables, and website body copy. Regular 400 and SemiBold 600, with Italic for light emphasis. Matches the district's body text. It also covers accented letters, ñ, ¿, and ¡, so use it for anything in Spanish.

Installing Channel Islands
  • Mac: double-click the .otf file and click Install Font.
  • Windows: right-click the .otf file and choose Install for all users.
  • Websites and Wix: upload the .woff2 file as a custom font.
  • Canva or Adobe Express: upload the .otf file as a brand font.
  • Colors: any brand color works. Gold or white on Royal Blue, and Royal Blue on white, all pass contrast.
No accents

Channel Islands has no accented letters, ñ, ¿, or ¡. É, Í, Ó, Ú, Ñ, Ü, and Ç print as plain E, I, O, U, N, U, and C, so a word like AÑO would print as ANO. Anything in Spanish, and any name with an accent, must be set in Open Sans. It also has no < > ^ | or ~.

Important

Channel Islands does not replace the logo. Never type CHANNEL ISLANDS RAIDERS in this typeface as a stand-in for the word mark. Always place the official logo files. It is a display face; don't set paragraphs in it.

08 Name & Mascot

Writing the Name

✓ Do
  • Use Channel Islands High School on first reference in formal writing, then Channel Islands.
  • The team name is Raiders: capital R, plural. One athlete or the mascot figure is a Raider.
  • Write Go Raiders! or Channel Islands Raiders in spirit copy.
  • Include Oxnard Union High School District on official documents, letterhead, and forms.
✕ Don't
  • Write Riders or Pirates. Riders is an easy typo on rosters and flyers, and spell-check won't catch it.
  • Use a lowercase "raiders" or put the name in quotes.
  • Type the team name in place of a logo; place the word mark file instead.
09 AI & FlyersArtwork only · real logo · real fonts

AI Image Tools

Image generators can't reproduce the Raider exactly. Ask one to "include the Channel Islands logo" and it invents a new, slightly wrong pirate: a different hat, face, or sword, and made-up lettering. Use AI for the artwork only, then add the real logo and text yourself.

  1. Generate artwork onlyBackgrounds, illustration, or photo-style scenes. No logos, mascots, text, or seals.
  2. Leave clear spaceAsk for empty, calm space in a corner or along one edge where the logo will go.
  3. Place the real logoDrop the official PNG or SVG into Canva, Adobe Express, Slides, or Word.
  4. Add text by handSet headlines in Channel Islands and body copy in Open Sans.
✕ AvoidCreate a flyer for Channel Islands High School's homecoming with the Raiders logo and school colors.

The tool will draw a fake pirate and guess at "school colors."

✓ Use insteadGenerate a bright, photographic stadium-at-night background in deep royal blue #003DA5 with warm gold #F2A900 light. Leave empty negative space in the upper third. No text, no logos, no mascots.

Hex codes, not color names. Then add the logo and words yourself.

  • Never ask an AI tool to draw, recreate, or "improve" the Raider, the ship, or the monogram.
  • Never upload the logo as a reference image for an AI tool to imitate.
  • Confirm clear space and contrast before you publish.
